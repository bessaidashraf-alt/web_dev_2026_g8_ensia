Unipedia – University Residence Services Platform

1. Project Name and Description

Project Name: Unipedia

Description:

Unipedia is a web-based platform designed to improve daily life in university residences by bringing useful student services together in one place. It allows students to report maintenance problems, manage money requests between students, and exchange skills with other members of the university community. The platform aims to make residence life more organized, collaborative, and convenient by providing a central place where students can request help, follow up on issues, and share their abilities.

2. Problem Statement and Target Users

Problem Statement

Students living in university residences may face difficulties reporting maintenance problems, coordinating money requests, and finding other students who can help them learn or exchange skills. These activities may be handled through separate channels, making it difficult to organize requests, track their progress, and communicate clearly.

Unipedia aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized platform where students can access these services and manage their requests more efficiently.

Target Users

- University Students: Students living in university residences who need to report problems, coordinate money requests, or exchange skills with other students.
- Residence Administrators: Staff responsible for receiving and managing maintenance reports, communicating with students, and updating the status of reported problems.

3. Main Modules and Services

Unipedia focuses on three main modules: Fix It, Money Request, and Skills Exchange.

A. Fix It

The Fix It module helps students report and follow up on maintenance problems in their residence.

1. Problem Reporting: Students can submit a maintenance report describing an issue in their room or shared residence facilities.
2. Appointment Booking: Students can request a suitable appointment for a maintenance visit when an appointment is needed.
3. Request Tracking: Students can view the status of their maintenance reports, such as submitted, in progress, or resolved.
4. Administration Communication: Students and residence administrators can exchange messages or updates related to a maintenance report.

B. Money Request

The Money Request module helps students organize and track money requests between one another.

1. Create a Money Request: A student can create a request specifying the amount, recipient, reason, and relevant details.
2. Manage Sent and Received Requests: Students can view requests they have sent or received and respond to them.
3. Request Status Tracking: Students can check whether a request is pending, accepted, declined, or marked as received.
4. Receipt Confirmation: The recipient can confirm that the requested money has been received.

Note: The module is intended for organizing and tracking requests, not processing actual payments. Students should not enter bank passwords or other sensitive financial credentials.

C. Skills Exchange

The Skills Exchange module helps students share their abilities and find other students who can teach or assist them.

1. Publish Skills: Students can create profiles or listings describing skills they can offer, such as programming, languages, design, or tutoring.
2. Find Students: Students can browse available skills and find other students who offer help in a particular area.
3. Skill Exchange Requests: Students can send requests to learn a skill or offer their own skills in return.
4. Exchange Scheduling: Students can agree on a suitable time and format for an exchange, such as an in-person session or an online meeting.

4. User Profiles and Privileges

A. Student

Students are the main users of Unipedia.

Privileges:

- Create and manage their profile.
- Submit maintenance reports and track their status.
- Request and manage maintenance appointments.
- Create, view, and respond to money requests.
- Confirm the receipt of money when applicable.
- Publish skills and browse other students' skills.
- Send skill exchange requests and coordinate sessions.
- View updates and messages related to their requests.

B. Residence Administrator

Residence administrators manage maintenance-related requests and communicate with students.

Privileges:

- View submitted maintenance reports.
- Review and update the status of maintenance requests.
- Coordinate maintenance appointments.
- Communicate with students about reported problems.
- Mark maintenance issues as resolved when appropriate.
