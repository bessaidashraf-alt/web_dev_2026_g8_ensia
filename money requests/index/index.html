Money Request - Process Overview

How it works

Cash exchange around ATM trips

Unipedia helps students coordinate cash exchange. The platform tracks each step — it never moves money.

1

ATM student - Ahmed

Announces an ATM trip

Shares the ATM location, date, time, maximum amount, and CCP number. The CCP remains hidden.

Trip published
2

Student needing cash - Sara

Sends a request

Chooses the trip and requests an amount, for example 2,000 DA.

Pending
3

ATM student - Ahmed

Accepts or rejects

If accepted, Ahmed's CCP number becomes visible to Sara only.

Accepted / Rejected
4

Student needing cash - Sara

Sends the transfer

Sara transfers the money outside Unipedia, then clicks "I sent the transfer."

Transfer sent
5

ATM student - Ahmed

Withdraws and hands over cash

Ahmed withdraws the cash at the ATM and gives it to Sara in person.

Waiting for confirmation
6

Student needing cash - Sara

Confirms cash received

Sara clicks "Cash received." Ahmed is then asked to confirm the handover.

Requester confirmed
7

ATM student - Ahmed

Confirms handover

Ahmed clicks "I handed over the cash." The request closes after both students confirm.

Confirmed - Closed

Privacy & Safety

The CCP number is visible only to the student whose request was accepted. Never share passwords, PINs, or card codes.

Cancellation

Sara can cancel only while the request is Pending. After Ahmed accepts, it can no longer be cancelled.

Status flow: Pending → Accepted → Transfer sent → Cash received → Handover confirmed → Confirmed
