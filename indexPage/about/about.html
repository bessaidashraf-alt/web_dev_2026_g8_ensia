About Unipedia

Unipedia is a web platform for university-residence students. It brings maintenance reports, ATM cash requests and skill exchange into one organized place, and gives residence staff a focused maintenance tool.

What Unipedia is for

Four goals shape every page of the platform.

Visible maintenance

Every report has an owner, a status and a history, so you can follow it from New to Resolved.

Clear cash requests

Requests around ATM trips follow explicit steps, while the payment itself stays outside Unipedia.

Peers who teach

Find students who can teach a skill you want to learn, or share one of your own.

Privacy first

Students and administrators have separate access, and private records stay private.

Who uses Unipedia

There are two kinds of accounts. New sign-ups are always students.

Students

Student
  • Manage their own account and profile
  • Create and follow their own maintenance reports
  • Request cash on ATM trips or announce their own
  • Post Teach or Learn listings and arrange sessions

Residence administrators

Administrator
  • Work in a restricted maintenance-management area
  • Review reports for their residence and update their status
  • Cannot see money requests or private skill exchanges
  • Cannot promote other users

What Unipedia does and does not do

Unipedia records steps and statuses. It is not a bank and not a chat app.

Unipedia does

  • Record workflows and statuses
  • Show who did what and when, in a timeline
  • Keep reports, requests and exchange details visible only to the people involved

Unipedia does not

  • Move money or verify transfers
  • Ask for passwords, PINs or card security codes
  • Offer real-time chat
  • Match skills automatically

Payments happen outside Unipedia. Never share passwords, PINs or card codes with anyone.

Your CCP number stays hidden. It is shown only to the student whose request you accept.
